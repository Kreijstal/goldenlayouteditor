Native atom/embedded-BBeB metadata profile follows calibre:

https://github.com/kovidgoyal/calibre/blob/19bd4cbd4e3be8de87360e835498db6d56b15bd9/src/calibre/ebooks/metadata/lrx.py
Local changes: bounded atom traversal (32/64-bit sizes), strict brand and unique bbeb payload, bounded zlib metadata, no recovery/catch-ignore, structured worker protocol, literal DOM metadata output. No book-content decryption or signature validation.
The original native metadata oracle is retained unchanged under scripts/native-lrx-oracle/metadata.py for tests, with its copyright/license.
